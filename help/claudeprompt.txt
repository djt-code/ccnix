claudeprompt: claude's prompt: a line from the keyboard with a shell's keys

  local prompt = require("claudeprompt").new{ history = "/.claude_history", pager = true }
  local line, back = prompt:read("Goal: ")

What claude (turtle/claude.lua) reads at its prompts: the goal, the next line after a reply, and the y/N confirms.  It reads through lib/readline, so the prompt has ccsh's keys (that file's header lists them): moving by character and word, cutting and Ctrl+Y, Ctrl+L, the history with Up, Down and Ctrl+R, and Tab.  As a shell has them:

  Ctrl+C
      with something typed, drops the line ("^C", a new prompt); on an empty line it ends the session, as Ctrl+D does; at a confirm it is no
  Ctrl+D
      on an empty line, ends the session; at a confirm, no
  Tab
      completes the prompt's commands (prompt.commands, a list: /new, /tidy...), shown in grey as ccsh shows them
  Shift+Enter
      the goal goes on on the next line ("... "); Enter sends all of it.  A paste brings its first line only
  Alt+Enter
      the same, for telnet, which sends no Shift (Esc, Enter)
  PgUp, the wheel up
      on an empty line, the pager (opts.pager)
  Ctrl+T held
      stops claude, as before (the error "Terminated")

The goals and lines typed are kept in opts.history (a file, the last 1000 lines), so Up brings back one from an earlier session; a goal of several lines is kept as one, its lines joined by spaces.  The confirms aren't kept.  Without lib/readline it reads with CraftOS read().

prompt:read(label) returns the line ("" for the end of the session), or nil and how far to scroll the pager up (opts.pager: PgUp or the wheel on an empty line).
