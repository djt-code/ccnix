autostart: at boot, carry on with the turtle's job

Usage:  autostart          what startup runs (setup puts it there)
        autostart --now
            the same, without the 5 second wait

Runs the program named by the setting ccnix.autostart (mine, farm, mow or autocompost; `sysctl ccnix.autostart=farm`, or `ccnix setup --autostart farm`).  A turtle reboots whenever its chunk loads again, so this is what keeps a job going.  It waits 5 seconds first: any key cancels, for the prompt.

  mine
      a tunnel it was digging when it went down: back to the last home if it's elsewhere, then `mine --resume`.  No tunnel yet: a new one, as the settings say (mine.width, mine.height, mine.length: 3x3, 32 long unless changed). A tunnel it finished, or stopped itself (out of chests or fuel): it says so and leaves it to you.
  farm
      a saved farm: home if it's elsewhere, then farm it.  No farm yet: learn the one the settings give (farm.width, farm.length), then farm it.
  mow
      a pass it was in the middle of: home, then mow.  Mowing on repeat (mow.repeat > 0): mow.  Otherwise nothing: the lawn was mowed.
  autocompost
      autocompost.

With init at boot (`ccnix setup init`), the job runs as a service of its own name instead (service mine: `autostart --now`, not started again when it ends), so what it prints is kept in the system log (/var/log/messages) and the console is free: `service status mine`, `tail -f /var/log/messages`, `service stop mine` (as Ctrl+T) and `service start mine` (picks it up again).  Stop the service before running the program by hand.
