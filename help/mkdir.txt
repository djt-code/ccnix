mkdir: make directories (POSIX mkdir)

  mkdir [-p] [-m mode] dir...

Makes each directory, whose parent must be there already, and which mustn't be.  -p makes the parents too, as needed, and says nothing of one that's there already.  -m is taken and ignored: CraftOS has no permissions.  Status 1 if any couldn't be made.  (CraftOS's mkdir makes the parents always and takes no options: `mkdir -p a` makes a folder called -p.)
