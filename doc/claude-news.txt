claude-news: what changed for claude (the model driving a turtle), by version, newest first

When a turtle's ccnix is updated, claude tells the model, with its first goal, the lines under each version since the one it last ran with (kept in /claude/.version).  Each version has a "== 0.51.0" heading; the short lines under it are written to the model, in its terms.  Lines starting "--" are notes for people, not shown to it.

== 0.51.0
Blocks of nine craft both ways with no recipe added (coal and coal blocks, bone meal and bone blocks, iron, gold, nuggets...): the T.addRecipe calls in your helpers.lua can go.
T.craftBatch(list) crafts many recipes with one stash and leaves the products in the chest (opts.take brings them aboard): your batchCraft can go.
T.craftPlan{[name] = n} works out the steps down to logs, the base materials and what's short; then T.craftBatch(plan).
T.smeltAt({x, y, z}, item, n) smelts at a furnace you placed ({wait = false} returns at once; n = 0 collects later): your furnaceSmelt can go.
T.fetch(name, n, {stay = true}) skips the walk back; T.inventoryReport() totals everything with each count's age; T.prepare{need = {...}, freeSlots = k} stows, fetches and checks in one trip.
T.build(plan, {order = "column"}) builds walls column by column for less fuel; a build stops with "circling: ..." when its fuel runs over 3 times the estimate (fuelWatch = false turns that off).
Route digs (moveTo/travel with dig=) refuse a cell with lava or a water source beside or above it: "digging X at c would release lava at c'". Pass liquid = true to dig anyway; T.liquidNear(x,y,z) tells you first. A dig under gravel returns a warning as the second result.
T.digCell(x,y,z) digs exactly one cell from beside it, refusing liquid or protected cells: your helpers.lua digCell can go. moveTo{digOnly={"x,y,z",...}} limits a route's digs to those cells.
Routes cross protected areas through air the map knows (doorways, halls); they never dig there. Don't raise limit for a "no route" through an area: read what it says walled it in.
T.inside({"keep","wall"}, fn) owns several areas, so a cell in two overlapping areas can be dug; nested T.inside calls add up. T.withUnprotected is the same.
T.trail() lists the last 200 cells you moved through; T.retrace(n) walks back n of them, skipping loops: your helpers.lua retrace can go.
The map no longer forgets cubes in protected areas or round home; T.map.pin(box) keeps other places; T.map.stats().over says when it can't shrink further.
T.harvest(..., {maxFuel=, timeout=, exclude={...}}) caps a dig job (info.spent is the fuel used); T.fuelFor counts scans of radius 8 or less as free.
The chat tool says something in the game's chat, or with ask = true asks there and waits for an answer from the chat or the keyboard; T.report.ask(question, opts) does it from code: replace your askPlayer with them.
You are reminded when 15 minutes pass without a word to the player in chat (the setting claude.remindChat).
keep(name, code) runs a helper and keeps it in /claude/helpers/<name>.lua, one file a helper, each loaded on its own: move what /claude/helpers.lua holds there, a helper at a time.
A call whose code makes a function local at its top level gets a note that it is gone with the call.
Each lua result ends with a status line: where the turtle is, its fuel and what the call used, free slots, and lava or water beside it.
Old tool calls' code and old thinking are cleared from the conversation too; the person can type /new for a new conversation (memory and files carry over).
T.cfg.maxHeight is 256 in a session (it was 16).
A lua call with review = true shows its code to the person at the turtle and runs only on a yes.
T.up, T.down, T.forward and T.back now return false and why ("maxHeight 16 reached", "blocked by X at x,y,z", "no fuel"): read the why.
T.keep, T.diggable and moveTo's dig and avoid take a list or a set of names, and globs ("*netherrack") wherever names are matched.
A finished job (its last step done) no longer rides on T.report's messages; T.job.clear() ends it.
T.cfg.autoDump tidies first, says when a trip fails, and turns itself off after two failures in a row; T.dumpJunk finds its way out of an enclosure.
T.refuelFromLava leaves lava in protected areas alone (opts.exclude names more).
The chest catalogue keeps one record per chest (a double chest was counted twice); T.where says how old each count is.
T.placeAt's failure says which approaches it tried and what each lacked.
T.inspectAt goes round what is in the way by default.
"no route" names the protected areas and doors that walled the search in: a bigger limit may not help.
T.fetch says its count once; T.smelt takes back its own furnace inside a protected area; T.setOrigin warns of saved jobs in the old frame.
print, write, io.write, printError, setmetatable, getmetatable, rawget, rawset, xpcall, keys and colours work in your code, and a print at the top of a helper file no longer breaks it.
